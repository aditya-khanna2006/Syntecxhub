## 📌 Overview

This project was developed as **Task 3** during my **Data Analytics Internship at Syntecxhub**.

The objective of this project was to analyze employee data and develop an interactive **HR Analytics Dashboard using Microsoft Power BI**.

The dashboard provides an overview of employee attrition and helps analyze attrition patterns across different employee demographics, job roles, departments, job levels, and other workplace factors.

---

## 🎯 Objective

The primary objective of this project was to analyze HR data and identify patterns related to employee attrition, including:

- Overall employee attrition
- Attrition rate
- Attrition across different age groups
- Gender-wise attrition
- Education-wise attrition
- Job role-wise attrition
- Department-wise attrition
- Job level-wise attrition
- Job satisfaction and attrition
- Business travel and attrition
- Overtime and employee attrition
- Key HR insights for decision-making

## 📊 Dashboard Features

### 📌 KPI Cards

The dashboard includes the following key performance indicators:

- 👥 Total Employees
- ❌ Total Attrition
- 📉 Attrition Rate
- 🎂 Average Age
- 💰 Average Salary
- ⏳ Average Tenure

### 📈 Visualizations

- 🍩 Attrition Count by Gender
- 🍩 Attrition by Education
- 📊 Attrition Count and Rate by Age Group
- 📋 Employee and Attrition Count by Job Role
- 🌳 Attrition by Job Role
- 📊 Attrition by Job Level
- 🎯 Overall Attrition Rate
- 📊 Attrition by Department and Job Role
- 📈 Attrition by Job Satisfaction

### 🎛️ Interactive Filters

Users can dynamically filter the dashboard using:

- 🎂 Age Group
- 🚻 Gender
- 🏢 Department
- 🎓 Education Field
- ✈️ Business Travel
- ⏰ Over Time

The dashboard updates dynamically based on the selected filters, allowing users to explore different employee and attrition segments.

---

## 📈 Key Insights

The dashboard is designed to help identify:

- Overall employee attrition levels
- Attrition patterns across different age groups
- Gender-wise attrition distribution
- Education field-wise attrition
- Job roles with higher attrition
- Department and job role-wise attrition
- Differences in attrition across job levels
- Relationship between job satisfaction and attrition
- Employee attrition patterns based on business travel and overtime
