## 📌 Overview

This project was developed as **Assignment 2** during my **Data Analytics Internship at Syntecxhub**.

The objective of this assignment was to transform AdventureWorks business data into an interactive and visually engaging **Business Analysis Dashboard using Microsoft Power BI**.

The dashboard provides a consolidated view of sales, customer, and product performance, enabling users to explore business performance across different time periods, countries, sales territories, customers, and products.

---

## 🎯 Objective

The primary objective of this project was to analyze AdventureWorks data and build an interactive dashboard that helps identify:

- Overall sales performance
- Sales trends over time
- Sales territory performance
- Customer distribution and behavior
- Product performance
- Top-performing products
- Product quantity performance
- Key business insights

## 📊 Dashboard Features

The dashboard consists of three analytical pages:

### 📈 Sales Overview

This page provides a high-level overview of overall business performance.

#### KPI Cards

- 💰 Total Sales
- 🛒 Total Orders
- 👥 Total Customers
- 📦 Total Quantity Sold
- 💵 Average Order Value

#### Visualizations

- 📈 Sales Trend Analysis
- 🌎 Sales by Territory
- 🏆 Top Products by Sales
- 📊 Sales by Year
- 💡 Key Business Insights

---

### 👥 Customer Analysis

This page focuses on customer-related analysis and customer behavior.

#### KPI Cards

- 👥 Total Customers
- 💰 Average Customer Revenue
- 💵 Average Customer Income
- 🔄 Returning Customers
- 📊 Sales per Customer

#### Visualizations

- 👥 Customer Distribution
- 🚻 Customers by Gender
- 🌎 Customers by Country
- 🎓 Customers by Education
- 💼 Customers by Occupation
- 📈 Income vs Sales Analysis
- 💡 Customer Insights

---

### 📦 Product Analysis

This page focuses on product performance and sales contribution.

#### KPI Cards

- 📦 Total Products
- 🛒 Products Sold
- 💰 Total Sales
- 📊 Total Quantity Sold

#### Visualizations

- 🏆 Top 10 Products by Sales
- 📦 Top 10 Products by Quantity Sold
- 🌳 Product Sales Treemap
- 📈 Unit Price vs Sales Analysis
- 📊 Product Performance Analysis
- 💡 Product Insights

---

## 🎛️ Interactive Filters

Users can dynamically filter the dashboard using:

- 📅 Calendar Year
- 📆 Month
- 🌎 Country
- 📍 Sales Territory
- 🏷️ Product Line

The KPI cards and major visualizations respond dynamically to the selected filters, allowing users to explore different aspects of business performance.

---

## 📈 Key Insights

The dashboard is designed to help users identify:

- Overall sales performance and trends
- High-performing sales territories
- Top-performing products by sales
- Products with the highest sales quantity
- Customer distribution across countries
- Customer characteristics based on demographic attributes
- Relationship between customer income and sales
- Product contribution to overall sales
- Business performance across different filters
